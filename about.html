About ALFA SYSTEMS

Weighing solutions with practical support

ALFA SYSTEMS has been operating for 5 years and is backed by 18 years of industry experience. The business focuses on weighing solutions and related services, with product sales and after-sales support as important parts of its work.

Our focus

We aim to provide practical equipment options and professional support for customers choosing, installing and maintaining weighing equipment. Customers can explore the catalogue, place an order for review, or send a service request directly through this website.

ALFA SYSTEMS is based at Palikkunnu, P.O. Pady, Kodaly, Thrissur, Kerala. Contact our team to discuss your equipment, service or delivery requirements.
